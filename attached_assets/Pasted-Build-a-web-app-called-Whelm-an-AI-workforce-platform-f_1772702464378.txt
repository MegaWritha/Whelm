Build a web app called Whelm — an AI workforce 
platform for solo founders and micro businesses 
who are overwhelmed doing every job alone.

TAGLINE: Your company. Fully staffed. Powered by AI.

CORE CONCEPT:
Founders hire AI workers the same way they would 
hire real staff. Each worker has a name, a role, 
a personality and a skill set. They do not just 
talk — they create real deliverable work, learn 
your brand over time, connect directly to your 
platforms and submit work for your approval before 
anything goes live. When you eventually hire real 
humans, those humans collaborate alongside your 
AI workers — the AI becomes the foundation, 
the institutional memory and the always-on 
executor that never sleeps.

ONBOARDING:
When a founder signs up they complete a company 
profile:
- Company name and industry
- What their product or service does
- Their target audience
- Brand voice — formal, casual, bold, warm etc
- Brand colours and fonts
- Connected platforms — Facebook, Instagram, 
  Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, WordPress, Mailchimp
- Goals — grow audience, generate leads, 
  improve customer service, build content etc

Every AI worker is onboarded with this information 
automatically. They know your company before they 
start their first task.

AI WORKERS — FULL ROSTER:

   builds branded Canva templates, maintains 
   your visual identity consistently across 
   all content.

3. Video Script Writer
   Writes scripts for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, 
   Instagram Reels and long form video based 
   on your content calendar and brand voice.

4. Copywriter
   Writes everything — email newsletters, 
   product descriptions, landing page copy, 
   press releases, pitch text — all in your 
   exact brand voice learned over time.

GROWTH DEPARTMENT:
5. Marketing Strategist
   Builds campaigns, writes ad copy, tracks 
   performance data from connected platforms, 
   sends weekly performance reports with 
   specific recommendations for improvement.

6. SEO Specialist
   Optimises all content for search engines,

   identifies content gaps and opportunities.

7. Community Manager

   to the founder for immediate attention.

8. Email Marketing Manager
   Builds email sequences, writes newsletters, 
   manages subscriber segmentation, tracks 
   open rates and click rates and reports 
   on performance.

OPERATIONS DEPARTMENT:
9. Customer Support Agent
   Learns the product completely, drafts

12. Project Manager

TECHNICAL DEPARTMENT:

14. Data Analyst

15. QA Tester

STRATEGY DEPARTMENT:
16. Business Analyst

    monthly strategy briefs.

17. Investor Relations Assistant

18. HR Assistant

HOW WORK GETS DONE:
Step 1 — Founder hires workers from the roster. 
Each worker is automatically onboarded with 
the company profile.

Step 2 — Workers proactively generate work 
based on their role. The social media manager 
drafts next week's posts without being asked. 
The marketing strategist sends a Monday morning 
campaign suggestion. The community manager 
drafts responses to weekend comments waiting 
in the inbox.

Step 3 — All work lands in the founder's 
Approval Inbox before anything goes live. 
Founder reviews, edits, approves or sends 
back with feedback notes.

Step 4 — Approved work is published directly 
through connected platform integrations or 
downloaded for use elsewhere.

Step 5 — Workers learn from feedback. When 
the founder edits a post or sends work back 
with notes, the worker remembers and improves. 
Over time the work requires less editing.

APPROVAL INBOX:
Central dashboard showing all submitted work 
across all workers. Each submission shows 
the worker name, role, task title, platform 
it is for and submission time. One click to 
approve and publish. One click to send back 
with notes. Urgent items flagged at the top.

HUMAN COLLABORATION LAYER:
When a founder hires a real human that person 
joins Whelm as a collaborator. They see all 
the work the AI has produced, the brand 
guidelines the AI has built, the performance 
history and the content calendar. They can 
take over tasks, work alongside the AI 
or supervise specific workers. The AI never 
leaves when humans arrive. It becomes the 
foundation the humans build on top of.

LEARNING SYSTEM:
Every worker maintains a learning profile 
for each company they work for. They track:
- Brand voice patterns from approved content
- Topics and formats that perform well
- Feedback patterns from returned work
- Company updates from the founder
- Platform performance data from integrations

Workers improve measurably over time. 
A founder using Whelm for six months 
has workers that require significantly 
less correction than on day one.

company dashboard not a chatbot. Workers have 
AI generated profile photos. The inbox feels 
like a mix of Slack and email. Navigation is 
simple — Dashboard, My Workers, Inbox, 
Analytics, Settings. Dark mode available. 
Fully mobile responsive.

The overall feeling is: I have a real team. 
I am in control. I am no longer overwhelmed.
